About Minesweeper
Minesweeper: The Bloomfield Expedition

Help Wren and her clockwork mole Tock clear the clockwork mines the Rustmonger sowed across five lands, field by field - every story field can be cleared by logic alone.

Classic with the original sizes and best times, a Daily Field, Zen, Rush, the Gauntlet and Versus against a CPU, powers, a tech tree, tile sets and seasonal festivals. Every game saves itself.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Minesweeper: The Bloomfield Expedition - Free Online Minesweeper with a Story

Clear mines across five lands - Meadowmere, Frostfen, the Ember Badlands, the Sunken Archive and the Rust Citadel - with thirty logic-proven fields, five guardian fights and cinematics, directly in your browser.

Features

  • Faithful Classic: Beginner 9x9, Intermediate 16x16, Expert 30x16 and custom sizes, chording, question marks, best times and streaks
  • No-guess fields checked by a logic solver for the story, the Daily Field, Zen and Rush
  • Guardians that rebury cells, freeze flags, blow smoke and knock flags loose while their fuse burns
  • Gems, chests and powers: Scanner, Sonar, Lantern and Shield
  • Daily Field, Zen, Rush, Gauntlet and Versus races against four CPU rivals
  • A 24-node tech tree, tile sets, flags and clearing effects to collect
  • Seasonal festivals, daily quests, streaks, 42 achievements, player levels and autosave
