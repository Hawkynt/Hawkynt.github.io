About Bomberman
Bomberman - Spark Quest

Baron Blackfuse stole the Spark Core of Fuse Village and split it into five Embers. Guide Pip through Meadow Gardens, the Frostbite Caverns, the Ember Foundry, the Neon Circuit and the Shadow Keep - 35 stages, five bosses and illustrated cinematics - or play Arcade, Zen, the Daily Challenge and Boss Rush.

15 power-ups, 10 enemy kinds, a 25-node tech tree, 13 skins, bomb skins and trails, 28 achievements, daily quests, a weekly challenge, play streaks and a seasonal event every season. Everything is saved in your browser and your run continues where you left it.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Bomberman - Free Online Bomb Game with Story, Bosses and Seasonal Events

Play Bomberman free in your browser: drop bombs, chain explosions, blast bricks for power-ups and find the exit across a five-world story campaign with boss fights and cinematics.

Features

  • Story mode: 35 stages in five themed worlds with ice, conveyor belts, teleporters and darkness, five bosses and comic cinematics
  • Arcade mode with endless arenas, chain and combo multipliers and a high-score table
  • Zen mode without harm, Daily Challenge with seeded modifiers and Boss Rush
  • 15 power-ups including kick, glove, remote, pierce, shield, time freeze, gem magnet and cursed skulls
  • 10 enemy kinds: hunters that path-find, wall-passing wisps, bomb-dropping imps, splitting blobs and charging beetles
  • A tech tree with 25 upgrade nodes including the Mega Bomb, dash and Phoenix revive
  • Skins, bomb skins and trails, achievements, daily quests, weekly challenges and play streaks
  • Seasonal events for winter, spring, summer, autumn and the year-end festival with their own looks and rewards
  • Automatic saving: continue your run after reloading the page
