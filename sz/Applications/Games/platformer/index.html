About Platformer
Platformer

Run, jump, wall-kick and dash through five worlds - Sunleaf Meadows, Glimmer Caverns, Frostfang Peaks, Cinder Forge and Skyspire Citadel - thirty levels in all, each world ending in a boss with three phases. Find three hidden gems per level, beat the par time and earn three stars.

Spend coins and gems in a five-branch Tech Tree, dress up in the Wardrobe, race your own ghost in Time Trial, see how far you get in Endless and take on a fresh course every day in the Daily Run.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Platformer - Free Online Jump and Run Game

Free online platformer game -- run, jump, wall-kick and dash through five worlds and 30 levels with bosses, hidden gems, a tech tree, time trials with ghosts, an endless mode and a daily run.

Features

  • Responsive controls: coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, corner correction, wall slides and one-way ledges
  • Five worlds of six levels - meadows, crystal caverns, frozen peaks, a volcanic forge and a sky citadel - each ending in a three-phase boss fight
  • Every level checked reachable by a bot that drives the game's own physics with the starting move set
  • Three hidden gems per level, secret walls, checkpoints, springs, moving platforms, crumbling blocks, crates and coin blocks
  • Seven kinds of foes: walkers, chargers, hoppers, spiky foes, flyers, divers and turrets, each reskinned per world
  • A five-branch Tech Tree: wall kick, dash, double jump, hearts, shield, coin magnet, gem radar, fireballs, stomp shockwave, skins and trails
  • Time Trial against the ghost of your best run, an Endless mode of procedurally chained stages and a seeded Daily Run
  • Painted parallax worlds, textured terrain, animated hero and foes, particles, screen shake and synthesized sound
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch controls; progress saved in the browser
