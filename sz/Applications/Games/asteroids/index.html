About Asteroids
Asteroids: Starfall

The classic rock-shattering shooter, rebuilt. Fly the Kestrel through five belts in the Starfall story, from the Kuiper Drift to the Maw, and bring down five titans. Chase high scores in Arcade, drift in Zen, take on the seeded Daily and the Boss Rush.

Thirteen power-ups, raiders and hazards, a 29-node tech tree, ship skins, trails and shot colours, achievements, daily quests, play streaks and seasonal events. Everything is stored in this browser and your run autosaves.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Asteroids: Starfall - Free Online Arcade Game

Play Asteroids in your browser: rotate, thrust and shatter rocks on a wrapping field, now with a story campaign, boss fights, power-ups, a tech tree and seasonal events.

Features

  • Story campaign: five chapters, thirty hand-designed missions, five titan bosses, cinematics and dialogue
  • Arcade, Zen, Daily challenge and Boss Rush modes with a high-score table
  • Thirteen rock types including seasonal rocks, seven raider types and hazards like black holes and lightning storms
  • Thirteen power-ups: shields, tri-shot, lance rounds, seekers, Sunbeam laser, orbit blades, chrono field, Nova Bomb, singularity and more
  • A 29-node tech tree, ship skins, engine trails and shot colours
  • Achievements, daily quests, play streaks, lifetime records and seasonal event tracks
  • Autosave and Continue, keyboard, mouse and touch controls
