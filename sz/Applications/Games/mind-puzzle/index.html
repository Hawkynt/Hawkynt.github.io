Mind-Bending Puzzle - Free Non-Euclidean Spatial Brain Teaser Game

Explore 17 surreal rooms with hidden rules, non-Euclidean geometry, perspective shifting, and reality-warp distortion effects. Inspired by Antichamber and The Witness, discover each room's secret through experimentation.

Features

  • 17 surreal puzzle rooms with unique hidden rules to discover
  • Non-Euclidean geometry: doors lead to unexpected rooms with non-linear connections
  • Perspective shifting through Normal, Flipped, and Rotated modes
  • Reality-warp sine-wave screen distortion when passing through portals
  • 8 interactive object types: fracture, echo, nexus orb, shadow, color tile, phantom bridge, clue, and goal
  • Solution-discovery glow highlights with particle bursts
  • Ambient particle atmospherics and floating text feedback
  • Persistent progress and high scores tracking fastest completion times
