# Fantasy Puzzle

An elemental magic puzzle game for the »SynthelicZ« Desktop. Guide a young mage across grids of wood, channels, chasms, ice and boulders, bending fire, water, earth and air to open the way to a glowing portal. 40 campaign puzzles across five realms, a daily puzzle and 60 bonus trials — every one verified solvable by the game's own solver — plus hidden runes, star ratings, arcane shards and a grimoire of upgrades.

## How It Works

Each puzzle is a grid of tiles — floor, wall, wood, channel, water, boulder, stone, chasm, ice, a goal portal and hidden runes — with the mage standing somewhere on it. The mage walks freely over walkable tiles (floor, water, the portal, runes): clicking a reachable tile walks him there along the shortest path, and walking costs nothing. A level is solved the moment the portal becomes reachable.

A spell may target any tile the mage can stand on or that touches such a tile. Each element does exactly one thing:

- Fire burns wood — the whole 4-connected wood cluster turns to floor in one cast. Fire also melts a single ice tile into walkable water.
- Water floods channels — the whole 4-connected channel network turns to walkable water.
- Earth fills a chasm to floor, or raises a stone wall on an open floor tile — never on the tile the mage is standing on.
- Air pushes a boulder: the mage stands on the side you point at and shoves it away; it slides tile by tile while the next tile is floor, and a boulder shoved into a chasm falls in and fills it. The push needs a reachable tile on the opposite side of the boulder.

Hidden runes are collected automatically whenever they become reachable — no cast lands on them. A perfect run collects every rune in the level.

Every cast costs one mana from the well, and the mana budget sits a little above the level's par, so wasted spells run you dry. Star ratings: one for solving, one for finishing at or under par casts, one for every rune collected.

The rules engine ships a breadth-first solver: every cast costs one, states are deduplicated by tile layout, walkable region and collected runes, and the first solution found is therefore optimal. The same solver powers the in-game hint, which highlights a tile of an optimal first cast.

All 100 levels were generated by `tools/generate-levels.js` and kept only after the solver verified them: solvable, par inside the slot's target range, every realm element used by the optimal perfect run, and a non-perfect route of at least two casts. The generator is deterministic — a fixed seed per realm and slot — so the shipped `levels-data.js` is reproducible. Campaign pars run 3 to 9 with mana at par + 2; the 60 trials run par 4 to 6 with mana at par + 1. Grids span 10x8 to 14x10 tiles.

Everything renders into a fixed 1280 x 720 logical view, letterboxed and scaled to the window with devicePixelRatio awareness; the board is centred in its area with tiles at most 64 px.

## Architecture

- `puzzle-core.js` — the rules engine and BFS solver: tile codes, level parsing, reachability, cast legality, state cloning and keying, `solve` and `hint`. Pure logic, no DOM.
- `levels-data.js` — the generated level set: five realms, 40 campaign levels, 60 trials, frozen and solver-verified.
- `tools/generate-levels.js` — the deterministic generator (`node sz/Applications/Games/fantasy-puzzle/tools/generate-levels.js`): builds chamber-and-barrier maps and keeps only solver-verified candidates.
- `puzzle-ui.js` — the shared drawing kit: one panel frame, text that always fits its box, chips, keycaps, meters and headlines.
- `tile-art.js` — cached tile and mage art: five realm themes, eleven tile kinds with four seeded variants each, and the mage in 4 directions x (idle, walk, cast) frames per robe color.
- `scene-art.js` — cached realm backdrops (one seeded 1280 x 720 painting each), the four element icons and eight spell-effect strips.
- `controller.js` — the game itself: loop, input, walking and casting animation, HUD, the title/realms/trials/grimoire/wardrobe/help screens, overlays, persistence and window integration.
- `tests/` — node test suites: `puzzle-core.test.js` (rules and solver), `levels-data.test.js` (re-solves all 100 levels at their par), `puzzle-ui.test.js`, `tile-art.test.js` and `scene-art.test.js` (drawing-call budgets against a recording fake canvas).

The page also uses the shared desktop libraries `../../shared/game-effects.js` (particles, screen shake, floating text), `game-audio.js` (synthesized sound effects), `menu.js` and `dialog.js`.

## User Stories

### Walking & Casting
- [x] As a user, I can click any tile the mage can reach to walk him there along the shortest path — walking is free and collects runes on the way
- [x] As a user, I can cast on any tile the mage stands on or that touches a reachable tile; the mage first walks to a standing spot and faces the target
- [x] As a user, I can see the spell as an orb flying from the staff gem to the target, with an expanding impact ring where it lands
- [x] As a user, I can see a pulsing border and the element's icon over a tile the selected element can affect, and footprints over a tile I can walk to
- [x] As a user, I can see a fading cross over a tile the pointer rests on when it can neither be walked to nor cast on, and hear a rejection when I click it
- [x] As a user, I can click the element buttons in the bottom bar to select an element; elements the current realm does not use show a lock

### Elements
- [x] As a user, I can burn wood with fire — the whole connected wood cluster turns to floor in one cast, with a "Chain x N!" callout for clusters of three or more
- [x] As a user, I can melt a single ice tile into walkable water with fire
- [x] As a user, I can flood a channel network with water — every connected channel turns to walkable water
- [x] As a user, I can fill a chasm with earth to bridge the gap
- [x] As a user, I can raise a stone wall on open ground with earth — but never on the tile the mage stands on
- [x] As a user, I can point at the side of a boulder to have the mage stand there and shove it away with air
- [x] As a user, I can shove a boulder into a chasm — it falls in and the chasm closes
- [x] As a user, I can only push a boulder from a side I can reach, and a push arrow previews the direction on hover

### Tiles
- [x] As a user, I can tell floor, wall, wood, channel, water, boulder, stone, chasm, ice and runes apart at a glance — each is painted per realm theme with four seeded variants so nothing looks stamped
- [x] As a user, I can see water tiles ripple with an animated frame
- [x] As a user, I can see the obstacle tiles up close on the Help screen's Tiles page

### Mana, Undo & Hints
- [x] As a user, I can see my mana as a row of crystals and a chip — every cast drinks one, and the well is a little larger than the level's par
- [x] As a user, I can run out of mana and get an "Out of Mana" panel offering Undo, Restart or Levels
- [x] As a user, I can press Z to undo the last cast, and the mana it cost comes back
- [x] As a user, I can press H to spend a hint: the solver highlights a tile of an optimal first cast for 2.5 seconds and the level's hint banner shows for 4 seconds
- [x] As a user, I can see my remaining hints and undos in the top bar

### Stars & Shards
- [x] As a user, I can earn one star for solving, one for finishing at or under the level's par casts, and one for collecting every rune
- [x] As a user, I can earn arcane shards for every star above my previous best — one per star in the campaign, two per star in the trials
- [x] As a user, I can watch the stars pop in one by one on the completion panel, with the cast and rune tally and the shard gain
- [x] As a user, I can open the High Scores dialog from the Game menu to see the ten best completions and reset them

### Campaign & Realms
- [x] As a user, I can play 40 campaign puzzles across five realms of eight levels: Ember Grove (fire), Tide Caverns (fire, water), Stone Peaks (fire, water, earth), Sky Ruins (all four elements) and Elemental Sanctum (all four)
- [x] As a user, I can feel the difficulty climb: pars run from 3 in the first realm to 9 in the last, and grids grow from 10x8 to 14x9 tiles
- [x] As a user, I can pick any unlocked level from the realm map; the next level opens with at least one star on the previous one, and a realm opens once its predecessor's last level is solved
- [x] As a user, I can see each realm as a card with a miniature of its scene, its star tally out of 24, and its eight levels as numbered nodes carrying my earned stars
- [x] As a user, I can watch a locked realm's lock shatter the first time it opens, and a "Realm Restored!" banner when I clear a realm for the first time

### Daily Puzzle & Trials
- [x] As a user, I can play a daily puzzle — one of the 60 trials chosen from today's date, the same all day — and collect 5 arcane shards for the first solve
- [x] As a user, I can see whether today's daily puzzle is already solved and with how many stars
- [x] As a user, I can play the 60 bonus trials (par 4 to 6) once I hold 10 campaign stars; the first six are open and each further trial needs one star on the previous
- [x] As a user, I can see the trials as a grid of 60 cards with per-trial stars and a solved counter
