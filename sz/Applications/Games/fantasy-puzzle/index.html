About Fantasy Puzzle
Fantasy Puzzle

Guide a young mage through 40 elemental puzzles in five realms, plus a daily puzzle and 60 bonus trials. Fire burns wood and melts ice, water floods channels, earth fills chasms and raises stone, and air pushes boulders into place. Walk freely, spend your mana sparingly, collect the hidden runes and earn stars for solving at or under par.

Win arcane shards and inscribe the Grimoire’s twelve upgrades, unlock new robes, and watch the realms come back to life.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Fantasy Puzzle - Free Online Elemental Magic Puzzle Game

Free online fantasy puzzle game -- guide a young mage through 40 elemental puzzles in five realms, daily puzzles and 60 trials, bending fire, water, earth and air.

Features

  • Four elements: fire burns wood and melts ice, water floods channels, earth fills chasms and raises stone, air pushes boulders
  • A walking mage: click to walk freely, and cast on tiles you can stand next to
  • 40 campaign puzzles across five realms, a daily puzzle and 60 bonus trials - every level verified solvable by the built-in solver
  • Hidden rune collection and a three-star rating: solve, stay at or under par casts, take every rune
  • Arcane shards and a twelve-node Grimoire upgrade tree: more mana, hints, undos and rune sight
  • Six mage robes - one yours from the start, five unlocked through play
  • Painterly realm backdrops, elemental particle effects and synthesized sound
  • Progress, shards, upgrades and high scores saved in the browser
