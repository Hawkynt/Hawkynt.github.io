Boulder Dash

The lamps of Emberfall went dark. Dig through the five depths of Mount Ember as Pip Ferrow, collect gems, outwit falling boulders, creatures and five bosses, and bring the light home.

Story, Arcade, Zen, Daily Cave and Time Attack modes, a tech tree, power-ups, skins, seasonal events and daily quests. Progress is saved in your browser only.

Boulder Dash - Free Online Cave Digging Puzzle Game

Play Boulder Dash free in your browser -- dig through caves, collect gems, dodge falling boulders and creatures, and reach the exit. Follow Pip Ferrow through a 40-cave story across five themed depths of Mount Ember, each with a boss, or play Arcade, Zen, the Daily Cave and Time Attack.

Features

  • Story mode: five chapters (Amber Mines, Frost Caverns, Glowshroom Grotto, Magma Forge, Crystal Heart), 40 caves, five bosses, cinematics and dialogue
  • Classic boulder physics: falling, rolling, crushing, pushing, grabbing
  • Fireflies, butterflies, moles, ice bats and spore moss
  • Magic walls, powder barrels, sliding ice, lava and brick
  • Eleven power-ups: dynamite, hard hat, hourglass, gem magnet, power drill, frost charm, lantern, swift boots, lucky clover, stopwatch, extra life
  • Arcade score attack with combo multiplier, relaxing Zen mode, a Daily Cave with a daily twist and Time Attack
  • Tech tree with 25 upgrades in five branches
  • Seasonal events with tokens and rewards, 16 miner skins, dig trails and tile sets
  • Achievements, daily quests, play streaks and lifetime statistics
  • Automatic save and continue in the browser
