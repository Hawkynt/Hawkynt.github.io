About Snake
Snake

The classic snake game grown into an adventure. Follow Sola through five worlds - thirty levels with golden eggs to find and five guardian bosses to free - or play Arcade, Zen, the Daily Garden, Time Attack and Guardian Rush.

Twelve power-ups, seven kinds of critters, ice, quicksand, spikes, lava vents, laser gates and portals. Scales from every mode buy upgrades in the tech tree and skins, trails and hats for your snake. Seasonal festivals, daily quests and streaks bring something new every day, and every run saves itself so you can continue later.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Snake - Free Online Snake Game with Story Campaign, Boss Fights, Tech Tree and Seasonal Festivals

Guide Sola the garden snake through five worlds to bring the stolen Sun Seeds home: eat fruit, grow long, collect golden eggs, dodge critters and hazards and free five guardian bosses. Or play endless Arcade with combos and stages, relaxing Zen, the seeded Daily Garden, Time Attack and Guardian Rush in this free HTML5 browser game.

Features

  • Story campaign: 5 worlds, 30 levels, 90 stars, 5 guardian boss fights, animated cinematics and a world map
  • 6 modes: Story, Arcade, Zen, Daily Garden, Time Attack and Guardian Rush
  • 12 power-ups: Dash, Time Warp, Scale Armor, Magnet, Phase, Molt, Golden Fangs, Fruit Rain, Quake, Frost Nova, Sun Shard and Venom
  • 7 critters: ants, beetles, hornets, hedgehogs, rival snakes, frost wisps and fire imps
  • Ice, quicksand, spike traps, lava vents, laser gates, crates and portals
  • 25-node tech tree in five branches, bought with scales earned in every mode
  • 20 snake skins, 14 trails, 14 hats and 8 board themes
  • 6 seasonal festivals with reward tracks, daily quests, play streaks, 30 achievements and lifetime statistics
  • Automatic save: every run continues exactly where you left it
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch controls; scales to any window size
