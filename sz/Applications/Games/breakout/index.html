About Breakout
Breakout: Prism Run

Fly the paddle-ship Aurora with Juno and her drone Bolt, break open the five brick fortresses of the Gloom Engine and set the starlight free.

Five more ways to play, twelve power-ups, a tech tree, paddle and ball skins and seasonal festivals. Every game saves itself.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Breakout: Prism Run - Free Online Brick Breaker with a Story

Break through five fortresses - Neon Gardens, Glacier Citadel, Clockwork Foundry, Coral Abyss and the Gloom Engine - with thirty stages, five guardian fights and cinematics, directly in your browser.

Features

  • Steel, titan, glass, wall, bomb, regrowing, gold and crystal bricks
  • Twelve power-ups: Wide, Multi, Slow, Fire, Laser, Barrier, Magnet, Mega Ball, Star Shower, Extra Life and two to dodge
  • Story: 30 stages (clear, gold targets, against the clock) and 5 guardians with shield rings and special moves
  • Arcade, Blitz, Zen, Daily Challenge and Boss Rush
  • Duo and Arena: co-op with CPU partner paddles on the other edges
  • A 25-node tech tree, paddles, balls and trails to collect
  • Seasonal festivals, daily quests, streaks, 38 achievements, player levels and autosave
  • Procedural soundtrack per fortress and mode
