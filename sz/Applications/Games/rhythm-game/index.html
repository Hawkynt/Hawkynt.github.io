About Rhythm Game
Rhythm Game

Hit falling notes in time with music! Use D/F/J/K keys to hit notes as they reach the hit zone. Timing accuracy determines your score: Perfect, Great, Good, or Miss. Build combos for score multipliers. Features 6 songs, final letter grades (S/A/B/C/D), neon glow effects, particle explosions, screen flash, BPM-synced background pulse, note trails, and an elastic combo counter.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Rhythm Game - Free Online Music Note Highway Game with Combos

Hit falling notes in time with procedurally generated music using D/F/J/K keys. Build combos for score multipliers, earn letter grades (S/A/B/C/D), and enjoy neon glow effects, particle explosions, and BPM-synced visuals across 6 songs.

Features

  • 4-lane note highway with smooth falling notes and timing accuracy grading
  • Timing judgments: Perfect, Great, Good, and Miss with visual feedback
  • Combo and multiplier system (x2/x3/x4) with milestone celebrations at 10, 30, 50, 100
  • 6 built-in songs with procedural music via Web Audio API
  • Final letter grades: S, A, B, C, D based on performance
  • Neon glow effects on lanes, notes, hit zone, and combo counter
  • Multi-layered particle explosions scaled by timing accuracy
  • Screen flash on Perfect hits and combo milestones
  • Note comet-tail trails and neon trail particle systems
  • BPM-synced background pulse with radial vignette
  • Mouse and keyboard input support
  • Persistent high scores per song via localStorage
