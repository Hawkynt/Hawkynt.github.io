About Rhythm Game
Rhythm Game: The Hush

A silence has swallowed the music of Hush City. Play it back to life with Lyra Vale and her drone metronome Metro, from the Neon Docks to the Crown Spire - every note is charted from the song you hear.

Free Play in four difficulties, Marathon, the Daily Mix, Zen, Boss Rush and the Remix Tour, power-ups and Surge, a tech tree, a shop with Record Crates and seasonal festivals. Songs in progress save themselves.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Rhythm Game: The Hush - Free Online Rhythm Game with a Story

Hit notes in four lanes in time with music composed live in your browser, across five districts of Hush City - the Neon Docks, the Glass Gardens, the Rust Foundry, the Undertow Club and the Crown Spire - with thirty songs, five boss duels and cinematics.

Features

  • Charts built from the music you hear: kick, snare, bass line and melody
  • Perfect, Great and Good judgements with Early/Late and audio calibration
  • Holds, chords, Hush notes to avoid and drift notes that slide between lanes
  • Star phrases and Surge, shield, heart, magnet and multiplier pickups, boosters
  • Five boss duels with telegraphed attacks: Bass Drop, Glass Fog, Hammerfall, Riptide and Silence
  • Free Play in Easy, Normal, Hard and Expert with practice speeds and song mastery
  • Marathon, Daily Mix, Zen, Boss Rush, Weekly Remix and the Remix Tour
  • Tech tree, shop, Record Crates, performers, note skins, highways and hit effects
  • Seasonal festivals, daily quests, streaks, levels, Fame and achievements
