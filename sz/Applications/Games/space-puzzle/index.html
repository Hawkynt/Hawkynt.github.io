Space Puzzle

A gravity-manipulation puzzle game where you drag and launch a probe through gravity wells and asteroid fields to reach repair goals. Navigate 12 handcrafted levels of increasing complexity, earn star ratings based on your moves and time, and repair your spacecraft one component at a time.

Space Puzzle - Free Online Gravity Manipulation Physics Puzzle Game

Launch probes through gravity wells and asteroid fields to repair your stranded spacecraft in this free browser-based physics puzzle game with 12 handcrafted levels and star ratings.

Features

  • Gravity wells with real-time gravitational physics and visual ripple effects
  • Intuitive drag-and-launch mechanic with variable force
  • 12 handcrafted levels with progressive difficulty
  • Asteroid fields with bounce physics, impact particles, and screen shake
  • Spacecraft repair objectives across all 12 levels
  • Star rating system based on moves vs par and elapsed time
  • Glow trail effects on launched probes navigating through space
  • Animated gravity well distortion with ripple rings and radial glow
  • Particle cascade celebrations on level completion
  • Floating score text and screen shake feedback
  • Twinkling starfield background for deep-space atmosphere
  • High score persistence across sessions via localStorage
