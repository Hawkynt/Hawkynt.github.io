About Physics Puzzle
Physics Puzzle

Build a contraption from planks, steel blocks, springs, bumpers, fans, conveyors and magnets, then pull back the launcher and light every target. Forty levels in five worlds - Tinker Workshop, Greenhouse Garden, Cryo Lab, Volcano Forge and the low-gravity Orbit Station - with glass, TNT, portals, water, lava, gears and pendulums. Earn three stars per level: solve it, beat the par and grab every star.

Spend coins in a five-branch Tech Tree, take on ten Trick Shots, a new Daily Challenge every day, or build freely in the Sandbox. Every level is proven solvable by replaying a stored solution with the game's own deterministic physics.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Physics Puzzle - Free Online Build and Launch Physics Game

Free online physics puzzle game -- build with planks, springs, fans and magnets, then aim and launch to light every target across five worlds and 40 levels, with trick shots, a daily challenge, a sandbox and a tech tree.

Features

  • A real rigid-body physics engine: circles and polygons, friction, restitution, stacking, sleeping bodies and a fixed, deterministic time step
  • Seven placeable parts: planks, steel blocks, spring pads, bumpers, fans, conveyors and magnets, snapped to a grid and turned in 15 degree steps
  • Five worlds of eight levels: Tinker Workshop, Greenhouse Garden, Cryo Lab, Volcano Forge and the low-gravity Orbit Station
  • Gadgets: breakable glass, chain-reacting TNT, portals, water, lava, ice, spinning gears, swinging hammers, pendulums, crates and boulders
  • Three stars per level - solve it, beat the par, collect every star in the air - and every level verified solvable by a stored solution replay
  • Trick Shots, a seeded Daily Challenge and a free-building Sandbox
  • A five-branch Tech Tree: launcher power, aiming guides, ricochet preview, multi-ball, spare parts, blueprints, bullet time, coin bonuses, ball skins and trails
  • Painted backdrops, textured materials, sparks, glass shards, explosions, slow motion on near misses, confetti and synthesized sound
  • Mouse, touch and keyboard controls; progress saved in the browser
