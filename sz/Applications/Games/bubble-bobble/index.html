About Bubble Bobble
Bubble Bobble: Fizz and the Dry Lagoon

Help Fizz the bubble newt win back the water Baron Dryspell stole from five kingdoms: trap his goons in bubbles and pop them in chains.

Five more ways to play, elemental bubbles, candy power-ups, a tech tree, newt colours and seasonal festivals. Every game saves itself.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Bubble Bobble: Fizz and the Dry Lagoon - Free Online Bubble Platformer with a Story

Trap enemies in bubbles across five kingdoms - Candy Caverns, Toybox Tower, Canopy Kingdom, Cloudworks and Dryspell Castle - with thirty rooms, five guardian fights and cinematics, directly in your browser.

Features

  • Trap and pop enemies, chain pops worth double each, bounce on bubbles, wrap through the floor
  • Seven enemy kinds, hurry-up and a shade that hunts slow players
  • Water, lightning, fire and star bubbles; eight candy power-ups
  • Arcade, Blitz, Zen, Daily Challenge and Boss Rush
  • Co-op: Pop joins as a CPU partner in every mode and story room
  • A 25-node tech tree, newt colours, bubbles and pop trails to collect
  • Seasonal festivals, daily quests, streaks, 37 achievements, player levels and autosave
