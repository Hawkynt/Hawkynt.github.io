About Space Mining
Space Mining

Fly a mining ship out of Kessler Station into five asteroid belts. Laser rocks until they crack into smaller rocks and ore, reel the nuggets in with the tractor beam and haul them home past volatile rocks, radiation, solar storms, gravity wells and pirates.

Sell ten ores into a market that moves, smelt bars, fill contracts, buy four hulls and upgrades that level up, build five station modules, deploy mining drones and research a 32-node tech tree - all the way to the Crown Heart. Plus Gold Rush and the seeded Daily Belt.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Space Mining - Free Online Asteroid Mining and Economy Game with Tech Tree, Drones and Five Belts

Fly a mining ship through large scrolling asteroid belts, laser asteroids apart into ore, tractor the nuggets aboard and sell them in a living market at your home station in this free HTML5 browser game.

Features

  • 3 game modes: Career, Gold Rush (five minutes) and a seeded Daily Belt
  • 5 asteroid belts with their own scenery, ore and hazards: Kessler Drift, Amber Reach, Verdant Shoals, Crimson Maw and Void Crown
  • 10 ores from iron and ice to platinum, iridium and void crystal
  • Asteroids that crack into smaller rocks and ore nuggets, with chunks, dust and shockwaves
  • Inertia flight, boost, tractor beam, scanner pulse, seismic charges and mining drones
  • Hazards: volatile chain-reacting rocks, radiation fields, solar storms, gravity wells and three kinds of pirates
  • Market prices that drift with booms, busts and your own sales, refining into bars and delivery contracts
  • 4 hulls, 6 upgrades that level up to 10, 5 station modules that change the station
  • 32-node tech tree in Drilling, Engines, Logistics, Automation, Defense and Science
  • Geodes, derelicts and the Crown Heart; 17 achievements
  • Cinematics: launch intro, belt arrival, docking, discoveries, new belts and the ending
  • Touch controls with virtual sticks
