About Flappy Bird
Flappy Bird and the Season Feathers

Corvus the Storm Crow has scattered the six Season Feathers. Fly Sunny through six lands and 24 stages, beat six bosses in their arenas and bring the seasons home - with an intro, chapter scenes, victory scenes and a finale.

Seven modes: Story, Classic, Arcade (points, multiplier, FEVER), Zen (nothing hurts), Time Attack, a seeded Daily Challenge with a daily twist, and Boss Rush. Twelve power-ups, storm crows, a Nest tech tree with 27 upgrades in six branches, and a wardrobe of 14 birds, 14 hats, 12 trails and seasonal scenery themes.

Seasonal events change the scenery and fill a reward track; daily quests, a weekly challenge, a day streak and 37 achievements keep you coming back. Everything is saved in your browser, and a run in flight resumes with Continue.

Flappy Bird and the Season Feathers - Free Online Browser Game with Story, Bosses, Arcade and Zen Modes

Tap to flap and guide Sunny through pipes that move, close and carry lasers. Corvus the Storm Crow has scattered the six Season Feathers across six lands; recover them in a story of 24 stages with six boss fights and cinematic scenes, or play Classic, Arcade, Zen, Time Attack, the Daily Challenge and Boss Rush.

Features

  • One-button gameplay with keyboard, mouse and touch: flap, glide, and pause with the on-screen button
  • Story mode: six chapters (Sunny Meadow, Dune Canyon, Frost Peaks, Twilight Woods, Neon City, Ember Volcano), a world map, intro, chapter, victory and finale cinematics
  • Six bosses with their own attacks: Queen Buzzella, Dunejaw, Glacius, Duskmoth, MK-7 Crowbot and Corvus - strike back with sun orbs
  • Arcade score attack with a multiplier, FEVER and a top-10 table; Zen mode where nothing hurts and every pipe rings a chime
  • Classic with medals, 60-second Time Attack, a Daily Challenge seeded by the date with a daily twist, and Boss Rush
  • Twelve power-ups: shield, magnet, slow-mo, ghost, double coins, tiny, Rocket Beak, Sky Rush, extra heart, Frost Time, Gold Rush and Wingmate
  • Hazards: wind gusts, icicles, bats, lasers, fireballs and diving storm crows
  • Nest tech tree with 27 upgrades in six branches, reachable from the title and the pause menu
  • Wardrobe with 14 birds (with perks), 14 hats, 12 trails and seasonal scenery themes to keep all year, unlocked with coins, achievements, story progress and seasonal events
  • Seasonal events through the year (Winter Wonderflap, Year-End Fireworks, Blossom Festival, Summer Splash, Harvest Moon, Spooky Flight) with tokens and a reward track
  • Daily quests, a weekly featured challenge, a day streak bonus, 37 achievements and lifetime statistics
  • Autosave: the run in flight is saved locally and resumes with Continue, even after a reload
  • Particles, screen shake, hit-stop, squash and stretch, flashes and synthesized sound for every event
