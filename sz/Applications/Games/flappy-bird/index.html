Flappy Bird - Free Online Browser Game with Four Modes, Six Biomes and a Nest Tech Tree

Tap to flap and guide a bird through gates that move, close and hide hazards. Play Adventure across 24 stages in six worlds, an endless Classic run, a 60-second Time Attack or a Daily Run whose course is seeded from the date. Collect coins, keep a perfect-pass combo alive, and spend your earnings on upgrades and new birds.

Features

  • One-button gameplay: tap Space or click to flap, hold while falling to glide once the Glide upgrade is learned
  • Four modes: Adventure (24 stages, six worlds, 72 stars), Classic with medals, 60-second Time Attack, and a seeded Daily Run
  • Six biomes with their own sky, parallax scenery, gate style and weather: Sunny Meadow, Dune Canyon, Frost Peaks, Twilight Woods, Neon City and Ember Volcano
  • Biome hazards: wind gusts, falling icicles, bats, pulsing laser beams and ground fireballs, plus gates that slide or close
  • Coins in every gap, PERFECT passes that build a combo paying x2, x3 and x4, and CLOSE bonus coins for a near miss
  • Six power-ups: shield, coin magnet, slow motion, ghost, double coins and a tiny bird with a smaller hitbox
  • Hearts, invulnerability after a hit and a Phoenix Feather revive instead of one-hit death
  • Nest tech tree with 15 upgrades in five branches, bought with banked coins
  • Aviary with six birds, each with a perk, unlocked by coins or by clearing stages
  • Feathers, sparkles, coin rings, afterimage trails, screen shake, hit-stop and confetti on a new best
  • Synthesized sound effects with a remembered mute switch
  • Persistent save of coins, stars, bests, upgrades and birds, plus a Classic top-5 high-score table
