About Pac-Man
Pac-Man

The Hollow King has swallowed the five Lumens that light Pac-Land. With Pip, the last spark, Pac-Man eats his way through Neon Downtown, Sugarcane Gardens, Glacier Grid, Hollow Woods and Ember Core - twenty mazes and five bosses.

Plus Arcade score attack, a relaxing Zen mode, a seeded Daily Maze and Boss Rush; twelve power-ups, seven kinds of ghost, a 25-node tech tree, skins, trails, maze themes, daily quests, achievements and seasonal events. Your run saves itself.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Pac-Man - Free Online Maze Chase with a Story Campaign, Bosses, Power-ups, Tech Tree and Seasonal Events

Play Pac-Man in your browser: eat every dot, turn the tables with power pellets and relight the five Lumens of Pac-Land across a story campaign with cinematics, boss fights and a world map. No sign-up - progress is saved in your browser and runs continue where you left off.

Features

  • Story mode: 5 worlds with 4 mazes and a boss each, chapter cinematics and dialogue
  • Arcade score attack with a multiplier and dot chains, a relaxing Zen mode, the seeded Daily Maze and Boss Rush
  • 12 power-ups: Turbo Sneakers, Freeze Ray, Double Points, Dot Magnet, Bubble Shield, Ghost Walk, Pellet Blaster, Boo Bomb, Mega Chomp, Decoy Pac, Time Warp, Extra Heart
  • 7 ghosts: Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde with authentic targeting plus the dot-eating Gobbler, the charging Dasher and the wall-drifting Phantom
  • Tile twists: arrow pads, taffy, ice, portals, darkness and fire vents
  • 25-node tech tree in five branches, 16 skins, 12 trails and 6 maze themes
  • Daily quests, a play streak bonus, 28 achievements and lifetime records
  • Seasonal events through the year with their own tokens, decorations and rewards
  • Autosave and Continue, keyboard, mouse and touch controls, resizable window
