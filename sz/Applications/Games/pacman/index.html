Pac-Man - Free Classic Arcade Maze Chase Game Online

Play the classic Pac-Man arcade game in your browser. Navigate the maze, eat dots, grab power pellets to eat ghosts, collect fruit bonuses, use powerups, and battle bosses across themed worlds with authentic ghost AI.

Features

  • Classic maze gameplay with dots, power pellets, and tunnel wrapping
  • Four ghosts with authentic AI: Blinky, Pinky, Inky, and Clyde with unique chase behaviors
  • Escalating ghost-eating points: 200, 400, 800, 1600
  • 8 world themes cycling every 4 levels with glowing wall rendering
  • Boss encounters every 4 levels with unique abilities and HP bars
  • 6 powerup types: Speed+, Freeze, 2x Score, Extra Life, Magnet, Phase
  • 8 fruit types with level-based progression from Cherry to Key
  • Dot chain combo system with confetti at milestone thresholds
  • Cruise Elroy speed bonuses for Blinky on later levels
  • Intermission scenes between worlds
  • Resizable window with HiDPI canvas scaling
  • Persistent top-5 high scores via localStorage
