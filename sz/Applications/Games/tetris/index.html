About Tetris
Tetris: The Spire of Lumen

Help Kip and the stone golem Ollo rebuild five crumbling realms and win back the keystones from their guardians, block by block and line by line.

Seven more ways to play, T-spins, combos, powers, a tech tree, block skins and seasonal festivals. Every game saves itself.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Tetris: The Spire of Lumen - Free Online Block Puzzle with a Story

Stack falling blocks in a story across five realms - Verdant Ruins, Crystal Hollows, Sunken Temple, Ember Forge and the Starlit Spire - with thirty stages, five guardian fights and cinematics, directly in your browser.

Features

  • Guideline play: SRS rotation with wall kicks, 7-bag, hold, five-piece preview, ghost piece, lock delay
  • T-spins, back-to-back, combos and perfect clears with big effects
  • Story: 30 stages (lines, score, dig, gems, survival, combo) and 5 guardians with special moves
  • Marathon, Sprint, Ultra, Zen, Daily Challenge, Boss Rush and Master modes
  • Eight powers from power orbs, a 25-node tech tree, block skins, frames and lock effects
  • Seasonal festivals, daily quests, streaks, 42 achievements, player levels and autosave
  • Procedural soundtrack per realm and mode
