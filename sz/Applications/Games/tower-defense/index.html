About Tower Defense
Tower Defense

Hold a campaign of twenty-four maps across meadows, swamps, desert, crystal caverns, tundra and volcanic wastes. Build sixteen tower families, upgrade them through three tiers, pick one of two specializations and master it. Earn research to unlock towers, drill them and grow your economy, defenses and abilities. The waves never stop: stay to grind or march on to the next level. Bosses drop relics and unlock towers.

Waves bring runners, swarms, flyers, burrowers, chargers, armored brutes, splitting slimes, regenerating ghouls, healers, saboteurs, hexers, phantoms, shield wardens, wyverns, juggernauts and seven bosses.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Tower Defense - Free Pixel-Art Tower Defense Browser Game

Defend a campaign of twenty-four maps in six regions with sixteen tower families, tier IV specializations and tier V mastery, a persistent research tree and active abilities in this free HTML5 tower defense game.

Features

  • Sixteen tower families: Archer, Cannon, Frost, Tesla, Flamer, Venom, Laser, Sniper, Spike traps, Gold Mine, Mortar, Storm, Wind, Missile, Beacon and Arcane
  • Two specializations per family at tier IV (for example Longbow or Volley, Siege Mortar or Shrapnel) and a mastery tier
  • Targeting modes: first, last, strongest, closest
  • Twenty-four maps in six regions with star ratings: forked, merging and crossing roads, keeps, lakes, bogs, mesas, chasms, ice and lava
  • Over twenty monster types with clear counters, elites and seven bosses
  • Endless waves on every map with best-wave records, a world-map journey between levels, and game speeds up to 20x
  • Ten boss relics and three towers won only from bosses
  • Research tree with Arsenal, Economy, Defense and Abilities branches
  • Airstrike, Deep Freeze and Gold Rush abilities
  • Code-drawn pixel art, explosions, weather and sound effects
  • Automatic saving with Continue
