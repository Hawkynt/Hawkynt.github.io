About Cookie Clicker
Cookie Clicker

Click a treat that grows through eight eras - plain cookie, chocolate chip, frosted, cupcake, layer cake, wedding cake, cosmic cake and a whole bakery planet - while eighteen producers bake for you.

Research a two-page tech tree, grow and breed plants in the Sugar Garden, ascend for sugar crystals and take on the Sanctum Trials, catch golden treats, play timed and daily Bake-Offs or relax in the Zen Kitchen. Your bakery saves itself and keeps baking while you are away.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Cookie Clicker - Free Online Idle Incremental Game

Play Cookie Clicker free in your browser: click a treat that evolves from a plain cookie into a cupcake, a wedding cake and finally a bakery planet, buy 18 producers with their own animated bakery scenes, collect 130+ upgrades and research a tech tree in this idle game on the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Features

  • Eight treat eras with transformation cinematics, era milestones and a small cast of bakers
  • Decorations you can see on the treat: icing, sprinkles, cherry, candles, gold leaf, starlight glaze
  • 18 producers from Cursor to Cake Singularity, each with procedural art and an animated scene
  • 130+ upgrades, golden treats with seven outcomes, click combos and stacking buffs
  • Two-page tech tree: Test Kitchen (recipe notes) and Sugar Sanctum (sugar crystals)
  • Ascension for permanent sugar crystal bonuses
  • Timed Bake-Off, Daily Bake-Off with a date-seeded modifier, and a relaxing Zen Kitchen
  • Seasonal events with themed golden treats, tokens and rewards; daily quests and streaks
  • Sugar Garden: plants that ripen in real time, give bonuses and breed 17 kinds up to a Crystal Rose
  • Six Sanctum Trials after ascending, each a permanent production bonus
  • Collection of treat skins, click styles and kitchen themes; 130+ achievements
  • Procedural soundtrack for every era, mode and season; save export and import
  • Autosave, continue and capped offline baking; older saves carry over
