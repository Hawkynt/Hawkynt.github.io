About Space Combat
Space Combat

Free-flight arcade dogfighting in large scrolling sectors. Fly a 30-mission campaign through five sectors - survival, assault, escort, defense and salvage missions, each sector closed by a multi-part boss - or play Endless Survival, the seeded Daily Challenge and Boss Rush.

Five ships with their own specials, six primary and four secondary weapons, ten enemy types, asteroids, minefields, nebulae, black holes and solar flares. Scrap from every mode buys ships and weapons in the hangar and 36 upgrades in the tech tree.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Space Combat - Free Online Top-Down Space Dogfighting Game with Campaign, Bosses and Tech Tree

Fly free-flight twin-stick dogfights in large scrolling sectors: a 30-mission campaign across five sectors with five multi-part bosses, plus Endless Survival, a seeded Daily Challenge and Boss Rush, in this free HTML5 browser game.

Features

  • 4 game modes: Campaign, Endless Survival, Daily Challenge and Boss Rush
  • 30 campaign missions in 5 sectors with up to 90 stars
  • 6 mission types: survival, assault, escort, defense, salvage and boss fights
  • 5 multi-part bosses with weak points, core shields and three phases each
  • 10 enemy types: raiders, interceptors, bombers, kamikaze swarmers, sentries, outposts, shielded frigates, cloakers, carriers and lancers
  • 5 ships with their own special ability: Overdrive, Phase Blink, Aegis, Nova Burst and Time Warp
  • 6 primary weapons (blaster, scatter, beam, rail, flak, ion arcs) and 4 secondaries (homing missiles, mines, EMP, drones)
  • 36-node tech tree in Weapons, Defense, Engines, Systems and Style (skins, trails, engine colours)
  • Hazards: breakable asteroids, minefields, nebulae, black holes and solar flares
  • Inertia flight with boost heat, dodge roll, radar and off-screen indicators
  • Explosions with debris, shockwaves, shield flares, screen shake, hit-stop and slow motion
  • Touch controls with virtual sticks
