About Space Farming
Space Farming

Casual pixel-art farm simulation on an alien planet. Grow 14 crops that love their own season, raise six kinds of space critters, and research a tech tree with Agriculture, Husbandry, Engineering, Science and Commerce branches. Reach goals, fill trader orders, and weather rain, snow, dust storms, solar flares, thunderstorms and meteor showers.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Space Farming - Free Online Space Station Farming Simulator Browser Game

Grow crops through multiple growth stages, tend livestock, survive space weather events, and sell produce for credits in this casual space station farming game playable directly in your browser.

Features

  • Pixel-art farm on an alien meadow under a parallax sky with planets, sun, moon and stars
  • 14 crops with growth stages and favourite seasons, from Space Wheat to Comet Pumpkins and Frost Kale
  • 6 livestock types including Moon Rabbits and pollinating Star Bees
  • Tech tree with Agriculture, Husbandry, Engineering, Science and Commerce branches
  • Goals with rewards, trader orders and a market
  • Seasons, day and night, rain, snow, dust storms, solar flares, thunderstorms and meteor showers
  • 14 buildings including sprinklers, greenhouses, wind turbines, grow lamps and pest zappers
  • Drag selection for batch planting and harvesting, keyboard and touch controls
