About Endless Runner
Endless Runner

The classic auto-runner grown into an adventure. Run with Nova the courier and Pip her lantern drone through five worlds - twenty stages and five guardians, two that chase you and three that fight back - to bring the Spark Cores home and relight the Beacon. Or play endless Arcade, the weekly Featured run, calm Zen, the Daily Run or Boss Rush.

Thirteen power-ups, jumps, double jumps, slides and dashes, near-miss combos, a 25-node tech tree, six runners with perks, outfits and trails, achievements, daily quests, streaks and seasonal festivals. Every run saves itself so you can continue later.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Endless Runner - Free Online Runner Game with Story Campaign, Boss Chases, Tech Tree and Seasonal Festivals

Run, jump, double jump, slide and dash through procedurally built courses in five worlds: Sunpetal Meadows, Ember Canyon, Frostpeak Pass, Neon Metro and the Hollow Rift - and when the story turns the way, run left, climb up or drop down. Outrun a stampeding boar and an avalanche, defeat the Dune Wyrm, Warden Mk-II and the Hollow King, and relight the Beacon in this free HTML5 browser game.

Features

  • Story campaign: 5 worlds, 25 stages, 75 stars, 2 guardian chases, 3 boss fights, cinematics and a world map
  • 6 modes: Story, Arcade, weekly Featured run, Zen, Daily Run and Boss Rush
  • 13 power-ups: Magnet, Shield, Double Score, Jetpack, Giant, Slow-Mo, Ghost, Rocket, Coin Rain, Phoenix Feather, Spring Shoes, Gem Rush and Shockwave
  • Fences, crates, overhangs, walls, spikes, saws, flyers, falling rocks, rollers, pits, ledges, springs and boost pads - every course checked for fairness
  • Story-driven turns mid-level: run back left over a collapsing bridge, climb a bell tower or a skyscraper facade, drop down a lava chimney or an elevator shaft - with their own scenery in every world
  • Near-miss scoring, combo multiplier up to x5, best-run ghost and a best-distance flag
  • 25-node tech tree in five branches, bought with coins earned in every mode
  • 6 runners with perks, 14 outfits and 14 trails
  • 6 seasonal festivals with reward tracks, 3 daily quests, play streaks, 34 achievements and lifetime statistics
  • Automatic save: every run continues exactly where you left it
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch swipe controls; scales to any window size
