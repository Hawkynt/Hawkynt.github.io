About Cooking Game

Build a restaurant empire from a food truck to a grand hotel: 30 career days across five venues, 12 multi-step recipes on the grill, stove, oven, prep board and plating station, plus Endless Rush and a seeded Daily Special. Cooking Game - Free Online Restaurant Time Management Game

Play Cooking Game -- Bistro Rush -- free in your browser: run five restaurants from a food truck to a grand hotel, juggle grill, stove, oven, prep and plating stations, serve 12 recipes and upgrade your kitchen in this restaurant management game.

Features

  • Five venues: Food Truck, Corner Diner, Le Bistro, Sakura Bar and Grand Hotel, each with its own menu and room
  • 12 recipes from Burger to Lobster, each a run of two or three station steps
  • Timing-based cooking -- stop the needle in the green zone for a perfect step, or burn the dish
  • Six customer types -- Regular, Kid, Businessperson, Tourist, Food Critic and VIP -- with different patience and tips
  • 30-day career with 1-3 star dollar goals, critic days and rush days
  • Endless Rush mode and a Daily Special seeded from the date
  • 17 kitchen upgrades in five branches: Stations, Gear, Service, Charm and Pantry
  • Combo tips up to double, built by consecutive perfect dishes
  • Illustrated interiors, chibi guest sprites, particles and screen shake
  • Synthesized sound effects with a shared mute switch
  • Coins, stars, upgrades and high scores persisted via localStorage
