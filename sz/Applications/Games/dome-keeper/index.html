About Dome Keeper
Dome Keeper

Hybrid mine-and-defend game. Mine sixteen rock strata by day and defend your dome from alien swarms at night, through moon phases, seasons and storms. Grow one upgrade tree with Dome, Mining, Movement, Weapon and Drone branches, find each site's Relocation Core and fly the dome to a new biome where the monsters are tougher.

Dome Keeper - Free Online Mine-and-Defend Strategy Game

Play Dome Keeper free in your browser -- mine by day, defend your dome from alien swarms at night, and relocate to new biomes. Upgrade weapons, dome armor, mining gear and drones in this hybrid action-strategy game.

Features

  • Dual-scene gameplay: surface dome defense and underground resource mining
  • Manual-aim dome turret weapon with mouse tracking and aim-assist
  • Destructible mine of 16 rock strata with 20 resource types, including six deep ores
  • Resource hauling and dome deposit mechanic with carry capacity
  • Day and night with moon phases, four seasons and weather: rain, snow, blizzards, thunderstorms and meteor showers
  • Six seeded biomes: Rocky Badlands, Dune Sea, Frozen Shelf, Alien Jungle, Ashen Caldera, Crystal Fields
  • Eleven monster types including two bosses
  • One upgrade tree with five branches, including courier, gun and repair drones
  • Relocation Cores, a landing and relocation sequence and rising threat per site
  • 4 primary gadgets: Shield Generator, Repellent Field, Orchard, Droneyard
  • 6 mine gadgets found in hidden underground chambers
  • BFS pathfinding for mouse-based underground navigation
  • Laser glow, impact sparks, mining debris, screen shake, and floating text
  • Nine-page help, local high scores, and parallax sky with sun, moon and stars
