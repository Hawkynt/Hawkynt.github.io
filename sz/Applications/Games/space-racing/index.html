About Space Racing
Space Racing

Top-down spaceship racing with drifting, boost and item combat. Race 8 themed tracks -- each also drivable in reverse -- through a four-cup career with championship points and trophies. Earn credits, work your way through the five-branch Workshop tech tree and pick one of five ships. Drift through corners for mini-turbos, tow behind opponents to fill the boost meter, and grab missiles, mines, shields, EMPs and turbos from item boxes.

Part of the »SynthelicZ« Desktop.

Space Racing - Free Online Top-Down Spaceship Racing Browser Game

Drift, boost and battle through 8 themed space tracks in a four-cup career: item combat, a workshop tech tree, boost pads, gravity wells, wormhole portals and AI opponents in this free HTML5 racing game.

Features

  • Four-cup career: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum, four races each with championship points and trophies
  • 8 themed tracks, each also drivable as a mirrored reverse variant
  • Drifting with blue and orange mini-turbos, a boost meter, slipstream towing and perfect starts
  • Item boxes with missiles, mines, shields, EMP and turbo, position-weighted drops
  • Workshop tech tree with five branches bought with race credits
  • 5 selectable ships unlocked by cup trophies or credits
  • 5 AI personalities with distinct racing behavior
  • Boost pads, hazards, gravity wells and wormhole shortcuts
  • HUD with position, laps, speedometer, boost meter, minimap and live standings
  • Procedural engine hum, drift hiss and barrier scrape sounds
  • High scores, best laps and a persistent career save
